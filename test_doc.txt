The Quantum Engine X-1 was invented in 2045 by Dr. Aris Thorne. It revolutionized space travel by allowing ships to exceed the speed of light using dark matter folding. The core component of the engine is the tachyon stabilizer, which must be kept below absolute zero using liquid helium. If the stabilizer overheats, the ship will be forced back into normal space instantly, potentially causing catastrophic structural failure.
